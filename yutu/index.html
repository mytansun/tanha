What is yutu?

yutu is the channel owner's private upload tool. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule Zoonk Shorts and manage the channel's playlists — on the owner's own channel, and only on the owner's own channel.

yutu is not a public service. Nobody else signs in to it, and it does not collect, store or share anyone else's data.
