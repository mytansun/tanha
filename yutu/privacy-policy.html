Privacy Policy — yutu (Zoonk uploader)

Effective: 16 November 2026.

yutu is a private tool built for the owner of the Zoonk YouTube channel (youtube.com/channel/UCcTJpe9DMN69qcGu3pgwbwQ). It uploads and schedules Zoonk videos and manages the channel's playlists through the YouTube Data API. It is not a public service: only the channel owner uses it.

What data yutu accesses

  • The owner's Google account and YouTube channel information, only as needed to authorise uploads (YouTube Data API).
  • The video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails the owner chooses to upload to the owner's own channel.

How the data is used

Only to upload, schedule and organise videos on the owner's own YouTube channel, at the owner's direction. Nothing else.

Storage and sharing

  • Authorisation tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are never sent to any third party.
  • No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for analytics or advertising.
  • No data about any other person is collected. yutu has no other users.

Third parties

yutu talks to Google's YouTube Data API only. Use of Google's services is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the Zoonk channel on YouTube.
